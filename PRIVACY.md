# TAT CCU 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 21 日

TAT CCU 是學生自行開發的非官方 App，與國立中正大學沒有隸屬關係。開發者沒有伺服器，不收集、不儲存、也不分享任何使用者資料。

## 存在裝置上的資料

- **登入資料**：eCourse2 的權杖、校務系統的登入狀態（Cookie），以及你選擇自動登入時的學號與密碼，存放在裝置的鑰匙圈，不會同步到 iCloud。
- **讀取過的內容**：課表、公告、成績、行事曆等資料會暫存在裝置上，讓下次開啟時能立即顯示。可在「設定 > 已存資料」查看並刪除；登出時會一併刪除。
- **小工具**：桌面小工具顯示的課表與到期項目，透過同一台裝置上 App 與小工具共用的空間傳遞。

## 會連線的服務

App 只在你使用相關功能時，直接連線到以下服務，資料不會經過開發者：

- 國立中正大學的系統：eCourse2、單一入口、CAS 登入及各校務系統（ccu.edu.tw）
- 圖書館館藏查詢（Ex Libris Primo，ccu.primo.exlibrisgroup.com）
- 公車動態（taiwanbus.tw）
- GitHub（api.github.com），只用於「關於」頁面顯示貢獻者，不會傳送任何個人資料

這些服務會依各自的隱私權政策處理連線紀錄。

## 相機

相機只在掃描課堂點名 QR Code 時使用，畫面不會儲存或上傳。

## 通知

課程與到期提醒由裝置在本機排程，不經過任何伺服器。

## 追蹤與分析

App 不含廣告、分析或追蹤工具，也不會在 App 或網站之間追蹤你。

## 聯絡

有任何問題，請在 https://github.com/KoukeNeko/TAT-CCU/issues 提出。

# TAT CCU Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

TAT CCU is an unofficial app made by a student and is not affiliated with National Chung Cheng University. The developer runs no server and does not collect, store or share any user data.

## Data on your device

- **Sign-in**: the eCourse2 token, the campus systems' sign-in cookies, and your student ID and password when you choose automatic sign-in are kept in the device keychain and are not synced to iCloud.
- **Content you have viewed**: timetables, announcements, grades and calendars are saved on the device so they show at once next time. You can see and delete them in Settings > Saved data; signing out deletes them too.
- **Widgets**: the timetable and deadlines the home screen widgets show pass through storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device.

## Services the app connects to

Only when you use the related feature, the app connects directly to these services; nothing passes through the developer:

- National Chung Cheng University's systems: eCourse2, the portal, CAS sign-in and the campus systems (ccu.edu.tw)
- The library catalogue (Ex Libris Primo, ccu.primo.exlibrisgroup.com)
- Bus arrival times (taiwanbus.tw)
- GitHub (api.github.com), only to list contributors on the About page; no personal data is sent

These services handle connection records under their own privacy policies.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan attendance QR codes in class; the image is neither saved nor uploaded.

## Notifications

Class and deadline reminders are scheduled on the device and do not go through any server.

## Tracking and analytics

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Contact

For any question, open an issue at https://github.com/KoukeNeko/TAT-CCU/issues.
