TAT CCU brings together National Chung Cheng University's eCourse2, the campus portal and the campus systems. One portal sign-in covers all of them.

This is an unofficial app made by a student and is not affiliated with National Chung Cheng University. The developer runs no server: data passes only between the device and the school's systems.

Home
The next class with its room and countdown, the rest of today's classes, assignments and quizzes due this week, and unread announcements and mail.

Timetable
The week's classes. Each class shows its next deadline, latest announcement and current grade.

eCourse2 courses
Sections, materials, assignments, forums, grades and attendance. Assignment progress (open, submitted, due, graded) and forum replies.

Messages
eCourse2 mail, announcements and discussions. Read, reply to and write mail.

Calendar
The school calendar (midterm week, holidays, add/drop) and assignment and quiz deadlines on one month view.

Campus systems
Grades, library holdings and loans, leave requests, payments and income, self-study space QR code and seats, enrollment certificate, event sign-up, bus times and countdowns.

Widgets and reminders
Home screen widgets show the next class and what is due. Class and deadline reminders are scheduled on the device.

Demo account
Try the app without a CCU account. Demo data is kept apart from your own account.

Privacy
Sign-in data is kept in the system's secure storage. Content you have viewed is saved on the device and can be reviewed and deleted in Settings > Saved data. No ads, analytics or tracking.
